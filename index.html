Product

Less data.
Better models.

Mendota is not a data vendor. It is one end-to-end post-training pipeline that audits, repairs and selects data, defines the RL environments, and tunes the training recipe for each model, so a small, verified dataset moves the metrics that matter.

  1. 01

    Audit

    Every sample is audited and continuously monitored. Leakage, contamination, broken labels and unverifiable answers are stopped at the door.

  2. 02

    Repair

    Flawed data is fixed, not just filtered: corrected answers, rebuilt tests, re-run verifiers. Good signal is recovered instead of thrown away.

  3. 03

    Review

    Repaired data goes through a second, independent review. Nothing reaches training without a check a verifier can reproduce.

  4. 04

    Select

    Model-aware selection: the right subdomains and difficulty for each target model's size and architecture, not one generic mix for all.

  5. 05

    Environments

    We define the RL environments, tasks and verifiable rewards, grounded in real-world work rather than toy sandboxes.

  6. 06

    Post-train

    The RL recipe is adapted per model, then the data flows into post-training. We measure the lift and feed results back to step one.

Rigorous by default

Strict auditing and live quality monitoring sit at the start of the pipeline, not at the end. Data quality is a guarantee, not a hope.

Small data, real lift

A targeted, verified slice beats a large generic dump. We optimize for gains per sample, not for volume.

Tuned to every model

Different models learn differently. We tailor both the data and the RL method to each one.
