Real machines. Real failures. Thousands of agent rollouts, every one reproducible. Build the ground our research stands on.

01About Mendota AI

Mendota AI is a research lab in San Francisco working on the gap between model capability and real-world consequence. We build rigorous benchmarks and environments that put AI agents in front of real infrastructure and real incidents, beyond the sandbox: InfraBench, BenchScope, and the training environments behind them. Our core researchers come from Harvard, MIT and the University of Washington, and include core contributors to Terminal-Bench.

02The work

  • Spin up whole worlds on demand: multi-node VMs and clusters, production-like state rebuilt per seed, faults injected on cue.
  • Own the harness that runs agents at scale: scheduling, isolation, traces, verifiers, results.
  • Make every environment fast, deterministic and cheap with images, snapshots, caching and smart scheduling.
  • Build the tools researchers live in, from task authoring to trace viewers and dashboards.
  • Keep it all standing as it grows. You build it, you run it.

03You

  • An engineer who has built and operated production systems, and lived with the consequences.
  • Deep in Linux. Fluent with containers, VMs and infrastructure as code.
  • Strong in Python, Go or Rust, writing code other people are glad to inherit.
  • At home with distributed systems on cloud and bare metal, and relentless when debugging them.
  • You care about reliability, and about the people who depend on your platform.

04Bonus points

  • Kubernetes, Ceph or other storage systems, networking.
  • SRE or incident response experience. You've watched real things break.
  • You've built evaluation infrastructure, sandboxes or RL environments for agents.
