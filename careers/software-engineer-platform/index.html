Build the platform that runs our benchmarks: real machines, real failures, and thousands of agent rollouts that have to be reproducible.

About Mendota AI

Mendota AI is a research lab in San Francisco working on the gap between model capability and real-world consequence. We build rigorous benchmarks and environments that put AI agents in front of real infrastructure and real incidents, beyond the sandbox: InfraBench, BenchScope, and the training environments behind them. Our core researchers come from Harvard, MIT and the University of Washington, and include core contributors to Terminal-Bench.

What you'll do

  • Build the infrastructure that provisions realistic environments for every task: multi-node VMs and clusters, production-like state rebuilt per seed, and programmable fault injection.
  • Own the evaluation harness: scheduling and isolating large numbers of agent rollouts, collecting logs and traces, running verifiers and shipping results.
  • Make environments reproducible, fast and cost-efficient through images, snapshots, caching and smart scheduling.
  • Build tools for researchers, from task authoring to trace viewers and result dashboards.
  • Keep the platform reliable as it grows, and own it in production.

What we're looking for

  • Strong software engineering experience building and operating production systems.
  • Deep Linux knowledge and hands-on experience with containers, virtual machines and infrastructure as code.
  • Fluency in Python, Go or Rust, and a habit of writing code others can maintain.
  • Experience with distributed systems on cloud and bare metal, and a talent for debugging them.
  • Ownership, and care for reliability and developer experience.

Nice to have

  • Kubernetes, storage systems such as Ceph, or networking.
  • SRE or incident response experience.
  • Experience with evaluation infrastructure, sandboxes or RL environments for AI agents.
