Define how the field measures what AI agents can actually do in the real world, and turn those measurements into better agents.

About Mendota AI

Mendota AI is a research lab in San Francisco working on the gap between model capability and real-world consequence. We build rigorous benchmarks and environments that put AI agents in front of real infrastructure and real incidents, beyond the sandbox: InfraBench, BenchScope, and the training environments behind them. Our core researchers come from Harvard, MIT and the University of Washington, and include core contributors to Terminal-Bench.

What you'll do

  • Design benchmarks and environments that test agents on real infrastructure work: incident response, releases, migrations and recovery, with outcomes that can be verified.
  • Build tasks, verifiers and reward signals for agentic RL that are hard to game and reflect real-world success.
  • Run large-scale evaluations of frontier models and agents, and dig into failure modes and what separates success from failure.
  • Study measurement itself: benchmark validity, redundancy, contamination and what a score really tells you.
  • Publish papers and release benchmarks, datasets and tools the community can build on.

What we're looking for

  • A PhD or equivalent research experience in machine learning, NLP, systems or a related field, with a track record of publications.
  • Rigorous experimental practice and strong Python; comfortable running and analyzing large experiments.
  • Hands-on experience with LLM agents, RL post-training, or evaluation and benchmark design.
  • Clear writing and the judgment to pick problems that matter.

Nice to have

  • A background in systems or infrastructure: Linux, distributed systems, storage, networking or Kubernetes.
  • Contributions to agent benchmarks such as Terminal-Bench or SWE-bench, or to RL environments.
  • Experience building datasets or tools that others have adopted.
