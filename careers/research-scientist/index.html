01About Mendota AI

Mendota AI is a research lab in San Francisco working on the gap between model capability and real-world consequence. We build rigorous benchmarks and environments that put AI agents in front of real infrastructure and real incidents, beyond the sandbox: InfraBench, BenchScope, and the training environments behind them. Our core researchers come from Harvard, MIT and the University of Washington, and include core contributors to Terminal-Bench.

02The work

  • Turn real infrastructure work into tasks agents can't fake their way through: outages, broken releases, failed migrations, cold recoveries.
  • Build verifiers and reward signals that hold up when a model tries to game them.
  • Run frontier models and agents at scale, then take the failures apart until you know exactly why.
  • Ask what a score actually means: validity, redundancy, contamination, signal.
  • Ship it. Papers, open benchmarks, datasets and tools people build on.

03You

  • A PhD or an equivalent research track record in ML, NLP or systems, with work you're proud of.
  • Rigorous by default. Strong Python, at home running big experiments and reading the numbers honestly.
  • You've built with LLM agents, RL post-training or evaluations, and have opinions about all three.
  • A clear writer with taste in problems.

04Bonus points

  • You've been paged at 3 a.m., or built the systems that page people: Linux, distributed systems, storage, networking, Kubernetes.
  • You've contributed to Terminal-Bench, SWE-bench or another agent benchmark or RL environment.
  • Something you built is used by people you've never met.
